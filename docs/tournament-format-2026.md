# HHC Basketball League 2026 playoff format

The Senior specification's original three groups of five was superseded by the organizer's instruction: **16 teams, four groups of four, top three per group qualify, all four group winners receive quarterfinal byes.** Existing team assignments remain in place.

## Senior

- Single round robin: 6 games per group, 24 total; 3 group games per team.
- Group rank 4 is eliminated. Ranks 2 and 3 enter the Round of 12.
- Group winners A1, B1, C1 and D1 receive byes.
- Game 25 (R1): A2 vs B3. Game 26 (R2): C2 vs D3.
- Game 27 (R3): B2 vs A3. Game 28 (R4): D2 vs C3.
- Game 29 (Q1): A1 vs winner 26. Game 30 (Q2): B1 vs winner 28.
- Game 31 (Q3): C1 vs winner 25. Game 32 (Q4): D1 vs winner 27.
- Game 33 (S1): winner 29 vs winner 31. Game 34 (S2): winner 30 vs winner 32.
- Game 35: semifinal losers contest third place. Game 36: semifinal winners contest the championship.
- **36 games total**, including 12 playoff games. No same-group matches occur in the Round of 12 or quarterfinals, regardless of which team wins.

## Junior

- Two groups of six; 15 games per group, 30 total; 5 group games per team.
- Top four per group qualify. Bottom two per group are eliminated.
- Game 31 (Q1): A1 vs B4. Game 32 (Q2): A2 vs B3.
- Game 33 (Q3): B1 vs A4. Game 34 (Q4): B2 vs A3.
- Game 35 (S1): winner 31 vs winner 33. Game 36 (S2): winner 32 vs winner 34.
- Game 37: semifinal losers contest third place. Game 38: semifinal winners contest the championship.
- **38 games total**, including 8 playoff games. No byes or Round of 12.

## Seeding and advancement

Game numbers restart for each division. All unique group matchups must be confirmed, protests resolved and qualification/seed ties decided before seeding. Only confirmed results advance teams. Tied final scores are rejected; overtime is required. Correcting results updates scheduled participants and clears affected future scheduled games; changes that would invalidate a started playoff game are rejected for organizer resolution.

Junior ties use head-to-head / mini-league wins, mini-league point differential, mini-league points scored, overall differential, overall points scored, then an organizer-recorded draw. Senior multi-team ties start with mini-league differential, followed by mini-league points scored, overall differential and overall points scored; two-team ties first use head-to-head. Tied subsets are recalculated.

No actual fixtures or scores are created by displaying the bracket. The organizer's existing fixture-generation, scheduling, score-confirmation and tie-decision controls operate on this format.
